15 Small Bathroom Storage Ideas

By HomeNest Editorial Team • Published September 22, 2026 • Updated September 22, 2026
```
```

A small bathroom can quickly feel crowded when toiletries, towels and cleaning supplies do not have a proper place. The key is to make use of the space you already have while keeping frequently used items easy to reach.

From vertical shelves to under-sink organizers, these practical ideas can help you create a more organized bathroom without requiring a major renovation.

1. Use Vertical Wall Space

Walls can provide valuable storage in a small bathroom. Floating shelves or narrow wall-mounted cabinets can hold toiletries, folded towels and decorative items without using much floor space.

2. Add an Over-the-Door Organizer

The back of a bathroom door is often unused. An over-the-door organizer can hold toiletries, hair accessories, cleaning supplies or other small items.

3. Organize Under the Sink

The cabinet beneath the sink can become much more useful with stackable bins or small organizers. Group similar products together so they are easier to find.

4. Use Narrow Shelving

A slim shelving unit can fit into a narrow gap beside a vanity, toilet or wall. This can provide extra storage without making the room feel significantly smaller.

5. Install a Shower Caddy

A shower caddy can keep shampoo, conditioner, soap and other shower products together. Choose a design that fits your shower and allows water to drain easily.

6. Use Baskets for Towels

Baskets can provide an easy way to store rolled or folded towels. They can be placed on shelves, inside cabinets or in another suitable storage area.

7. Add Hooks to Empty Wall Areas

Hooks take up very little space and can be useful for towels, robes and small bathroom accessories. Place them where they are easy to reach but do not interfere with movement.

8. Use the Space Above the Toilet

The wall above the toilet can provide useful vertical storage. Shelves or a suitable storage cabinet can hold extra toilet paper, towels and toiletries.

9. Keep Everyday Products Together

A small tray or container can keep frequently used skincare, grooming or personal-care products together. This can make the countertop easier to clean and maintain.

10. Use Drawer Dividers

Bathroom drawers can quickly become messy. Dividers help separate items such as toothbrushes, cosmetics, grooming tools and other small accessories.

11. Store Extra Toiletries Together

Keep unopened toiletries in one clearly defined area. This makes it easier to see what you already have and can help prevent buying duplicates unnecessarily.

12. Choose Multi-Purpose Storage

When space is limited, storage that performs more than one function can be useful. For example, a mirror cabinet can combine everyday mirror use with hidden storage.

13. Keep Cleaning Supplies Organized

Store bathroom cleaning supplies together in a suitable container or cabinet. Keeping them organized makes routine cleaning easier and helps prevent bottles from taking over valuable countertop space.

14. Declutter Regularly

Small bathrooms benefit from regular decluttering. Check toiletries and other products periodically and remove items that are empty, expired or no longer needed.

15. Keep the Countertop Clear

A clear countertop can make a small bathroom feel more organized. Keep only the products you use regularly within easy reach and store the rest in cabinets, drawers or baskets.

How to Choose the Right Bathroom Storage

Before buying storage products, measure the available space and consider what you actually need to store. Think about how often each item is used and whether it needs to remain visible or can be kept inside a cabinet or container.

The goal is not to fill every available space with storage. A good system should make the bathroom easier to use while keeping frequently used items accessible and unnecessary clutter under control.

Final Thoughts

A small bathroom can still have plenty of practical storage when you make thoughtful use of walls, doors, cabinets and unused corners. Start with the areas that cause the most clutter and improve them one at a time.

Simple changes such as adding hooks, organizing the space under the sink and keeping the countertop clear can make a noticeable difference without requiring a large renovation.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I add storage without making my bathroom smaller?

Use vertical wall space, the back of doors, corners and unused areas under the sink. Wall-mounted and narrow storage can provide useful space without taking up much floor area.

What should I store under the bathroom sink?

The space under the sink can be useful for cleaning supplies, extra toiletries, spare towels and other items that do not need to stay on the countertop.

How do I keep a small bathroom from looking cluttered?

Keep countertops as clear as possible, group similar items together and use baskets, drawers or closed storage for smaller belongings.

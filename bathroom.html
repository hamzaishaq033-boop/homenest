15 Small Bathroom Storage Ideas

A small bathroom can quickly feel crowded when toiletries, towels and everyday essentials have nowhere to go. The good news is that you can create more useful storage without making the room feel even smaller.

By HomeNest Editorial Team • Published September 22, 2026 • Updated September 22, 2026

Bathroom storage does not always require a major renovation. Small changes such as using vertical wall space, organizing under the sink and choosing containers carefully can make a noticeable difference.

Here are 15 practical storage ideas that can help you keep a small bathroom cleaner, easier to use and more organized.

1. Use the Space Above the Toilet

The wall above the toilet is often unused. A narrow shelving unit or wall-mounted shelves can provide a convenient place for extra toilet paper, towels, baskets and small bathroom supplies.

2. Add a Slim Rolling Cart

A narrow rolling cart can fit into small gaps beside a vanity, shower or toilet. Use different levels for skincare products, hair products, cleaning supplies or spare toiletries.

3. Install Wall-Mounted Shelves

Floating shelves can provide useful storage without taking up floor space. Keep frequently used items within easy reach and use baskets or small containers to prevent the shelves from looking cluttered.

4. Organize Under the Sink

The space beneath the bathroom sink can become a collection of random bottles and supplies. Use stackable bins, small drawers or removable organizers to separate cleaning products, toiletries and backups.

5. Use the Back of the Door

A door-mounted organizer can turn unused space into additional storage. It can hold hair accessories, toiletries, cleaning products or other lightweight bathroom items.

6. Choose a Mirror With Storage

If you need a new bathroom mirror, consider a mirrored cabinet. It combines two functions in one area and can hide everyday items while keeping the countertop clear.

7. Install Hooks Instead of Towel Bars

Several small hooks can sometimes be more flexible than one large towel bar. Place them on an unused wall or behind the door for towels, robes and other lightweight items.

8. Use Matching Baskets

Baskets are useful for grouping similar items together. You can use one for extra towels, another for toiletries and another for cleaning supplies. Matching baskets can also give open storage a cleaner look.

9. Add a Corner Shelf

Bathroom corners are easy to overlook. A compact corner shelf can create storage for skincare products, soap, shampoo or decorative items without occupying much usable floor area.

10. Keep Everyday Products Together

Instead of spreading frequently used products across the countertop, group them in one small tray or organizer. This makes the bathroom easier to clean and helps you quickly find what you use every day.

11. Use Drawer Dividers

If your vanity has drawers, dividers can prevent small items from becoming mixed together. Separate toothbrush accessories, cosmetics, grooming products and other small essentials.

12. Store Extra Towels Vertically

Instead of stacking towels in a tall pile, try folding and storing them vertically in a basket or cabinet. This makes individual towels easier to remove without disturbing the rest.

13. Use a Shower Caddy

A shower caddy can keep shampoo, conditioner, body wash and other products organized in one place. Choose a design that fits your shower area without blocking movement.

14. Use Small Containers for Small Items

Small containers can make a big difference when organizing items such as cotton pads, hair ties, clips and grooming accessories. Clear containers can also make it easier to see what you already have.

15. Declutter Before Adding More Storage

Before buying another organizer, remove products you no longer use. Expired toiletries, empty containers and duplicate products can take up valuable space in a small bathroom.

How to Make a Small Bathroom Feel More Organized

The most effective bathroom storage usually combines several simple solutions rather than relying on one large organizer. Use vertical space where possible, keep everyday products accessible and store less frequently used items in cabinets or baskets.

It also helps to avoid filling every available space. Leaving some open areas can make a small bathroom feel less crowded and easier to maintain.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I add storage without making my bathroom smaller?

Focus on vertical and unused areas such as walls, the back of the door, corners and the space above the toilet. These areas can add storage without taking much floor space.

What should I store under the bathroom sink?

The area under the sink works well for cleaning supplies, spare toiletries and other items that are not needed constantly. Bins and organizers can make this space easier to manage.

How do I keep a small bathroom from looking cluttered?

Keep frequently used products grouped together, remove duplicates and use baskets or closed storage for items that do not need to stay visible.

Final Thoughts

A small bathroom does not need a complicated storage system. With a few well-chosen shelves, baskets, hooks and organizers, you can make better use of the space you already have.

Start with the areas that are currently unused and organize the items you use most often first. Small improvements can make your bathroom feel noticeably more comfortable and easier to maintain.

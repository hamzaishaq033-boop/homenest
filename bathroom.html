15 Small Bathroom Storage Ideas

By HomeNest Editorial Team • Published September 22, 2026 • Updated September 22, 2026

Small bathrooms can become cluttered quickly because they often have limited cabinets, drawers and floor space. The key is to use the space you already have more efficiently and keep everyday items easy to access.

From vertical storage to simple organizers, these practical ideas can help you make a small bathroom feel more organized without requiring a major renovation.

1. Use Vertical Wall Space

Walls are often overlooked in small bathrooms. Suitable shelves, wall-mounted organizers or other storage solutions can provide useful space without taking up valuable floor area.

2. Add an Over-the-Door Organizer

The back of a bathroom door can provide additional storage for lightweight toiletries, hair accessories, cleaning items or other everyday essentials. Choose an organizer that fits securely without interfering with the door.

3. Organize Under the Sink

The space beneath the sink can become difficult to manage when bottles and cleaning products are placed randomly. Use suitable bins or small organizers to separate different types of supplies.

4. Use Narrow Shelving

A narrow shelf can make use of small gaps that might otherwise remain empty. It can be useful for toiletries, towels or decorative items while taking up relatively little floor space.

5. Install a Shower Caddy

A suitable shower caddy can keep shampoo, conditioner, soap and other shower products together. This can reduce clutter around the edges of the bathtub or shower.

6. Use Baskets for Towels

Folded towels can be stored neatly in baskets on an open shelf or another suitable area. Keeping towels together makes them easier to access and helps prevent loose piles.

7. Add Hooks to Empty Wall Areas

Hooks can provide simple storage for towels, robes and other items. Use them in suitable areas where they will not interfere with movement around the bathroom.

8. Use the Space Above the Toilet

The area above the toilet can sometimes provide useful vertical storage. A suitable shelving unit can hold extra toilet paper, towels or other bathroom supplies.

9. Keep Everyday Products Together

Group the products you use every day in one convenient area. A small tray or organizer can keep frequently used toiletries together while making the countertop easier to clean.

10. Use Drawer Dividers

Bathroom drawers can quickly become filled with small items. Dividers can separate cosmetics, grooming products, accessories and other essentials so they are easier to find.

11. Store Extra Toiletries Together

Keep backup shampoo, soap, toothpaste and similar products in one designated area. This makes it easier to see what you already have and can help prevent unnecessary duplicates.

12. Choose Multi-Purpose Storage

In a small bathroom, storage that can serve more than one purpose can be useful. For example, a suitable cabinet may provide both a surface and hidden storage underneath.

13. Keep Cleaning Supplies Organized

Bathroom cleaning products should have a designated storage area. Keeping them together makes cleaning easier and prevents bottles from taking over other storage spaces.

14. Declutter Regularly

Bathroom products can accumulate over time. Check your supplies regularly and remove empty, expired or unused products where appropriate.

15. Keep the Countertop Clear

A clear countertop can make a small bathroom feel more spacious. Keep only frequently used items within easy reach and store everything else in drawers, cabinets or suitable organizers.

How to Choose the Right Bathroom Storage

Start by identifying the areas where clutter builds up most often. You may not need more furniture; a few small organizers or better use of vertical space may solve the problem.

Measure available spaces before buying storage products, especially for narrow shelves, cabinets and organizers. Choose solutions that are easy to clean and simple enough to maintain as part of your normal routine.

Final Thoughts

A small bathroom can still be functional and organized when every storage area is used thoughtfully. Focus on vertical space, hidden areas and simple organization systems rather than trying to add too many large storage pieces.

Start with the area that causes the most clutter and make one improvement at a time. Once everything has a designated place, keeping the bathroom organized becomes much easier.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I add storage to a small bathroom?

Use vertical wall space, the area above the toilet, the inside of cabinet doors and other unused areas. Hooks, narrow shelves and suitable organizers can add storage without taking much floor space.

How do I organize a small bathroom without making it feel crowded?

Keep frequently used items accessible, store extra supplies together and use compact organizers that make good use of vertical or hidden spaces.

Where should extra bathroom supplies be stored?

Extra toiletries, towels and cleaning supplies can be stored in labeled baskets, cabinets, drawers or other suitable storage areas away from the main countertop.

25 Home Organization Ideas to Make Your Home Feel Less Cluttered

Simple and practical ways to organize everyday spaces, find things more easily and make your home feel more comfortable.

By HomeNest · September 2026 · 10 min read

ADVERTISEMENT

A home does not need to be large to feel organized. In many cases, small changes to where and how you store everyday items can make a noticeable difference.

The key is to create simple storage systems that are easy to maintain. Instead of trying to organize everything at once, focus on the areas that become cluttered most often.

Entryway Organization Ideas

1. Create a dedicated drop zone

Choose one small area near the entrance for everyday items such as keys, wallets, bags and mail. A small tray, shelf or wall organizer can prevent these items from spreading throughout the house.

2. Use hooks for frequently used items

Wall hooks can keep jackets, bags and umbrellas off chairs and floors. Place them where they are easy to reach when entering or leaving.

3. Keep shoes in one place

A shoe rack or compact shoe cabinet can help keep footwear together. If space is limited, choose a vertical design that uses wall height instead of valuable floor space.

Kitchen Organization Ideas

4. Use vertical cabinet space

Many kitchen cabinets have unused space above stacked plates, bowls or containers. Shelf risers can create another level and make frequently used items easier to access.

5. Group similar items together

Store baking supplies together, cooking utensils together and everyday dishes together. Grouping similar items makes it easier to find what you need and put it back afterward.

6. Organize food storage containers

Keep food containers nested by size and store their lids vertically when possible. This can make a crowded cabinet much easier to use.

7. Make better use of cabinet doors

The inside of a cabinet door can sometimes provide useful storage for lightweight items such as measuring spoons, small cleaning supplies or frequently used kitchen accessories.

8. Give your countertop a purpose

Try to keep only items that are used frequently on the countertop. Store occasional-use appliances and tools in cabinets or other dedicated spaces.

Bedroom Organization Ideas

9. Use the space under the bed

Under-bed storage boxes can be useful for seasonal clothing, spare bedding and other items that are not needed every day.

10. Organize your closet by category

Group clothing by type, such as shirts, trousers, jackets and occasion wear. This simple system makes it easier to see what you already have.

11. Use drawer dividers

Small dividers can separate socks, accessories, underwear and other small items. They are especially useful in deep drawers where items tend to become mixed together.

12. Keep a small bedside storage area

A bedside table or compact organizer can keep frequently used items together instead of leaving them scattered around the room.

Bathroom Organization Ideas

13. Use vertical storage

Narrow shelving units can provide additional storage without taking up much floor space. They can be useful in smaller bathrooms.

14. Group personal-care products

Keep similar products together instead of storing them wherever there happens to be space. Small baskets can help separate categories.

15. Make use of the area under the sink

The cabinet below a bathroom sink can often hold cleaning supplies, spare toiletries and other items. Small bins can make this awkward space easier to manage.

General Home Organization Ideas

16. Give every frequently used item a home

When an item has a clear storage location, putting it away becomes much easier. Start with objects that regularly end up on tables, counters or floors.

17. Use baskets to collect similar items

Baskets are useful for grouping items without requiring a complicated organization system. Use separate baskets for categories that make sense for your household.

18. Label storage containers

Labels can be particularly helpful when several containers look alike. Clear labels make it easier for everyone in the home to return items to the correct place.

19. Make frequently used items easy to reach

Everyday items should generally be stored where they can be reached without moving several other things first. This makes an organization system easier to maintain.

20. Store occasional-use items separately

Seasonal decorations, special cookware and other rarely used items do not need to occupy prime storage space. Keep them in less accessible areas when practical.

21. Use vertical wall space

Shelves, hooks and other suitable wall-mounted storage can help increase usable storage without taking up additional floor space.

22. Keep a donation box

Keep a small box or bag in a convenient location for items you no longer use. When it becomes full, sort the contents and donate or dispose of appropriate items responsibly.

23. Organize one small area at a time

Instead of attempting to reorganize the entire house in one day, choose a drawer, shelf or cabinet and finish it before moving on. Smaller projects are easier to complete and maintain.

24. Review clutter regularly

Organization works better when it becomes a routine. A quick monthly review can help prevent unused items from gradually taking over valuable storage space.

25. Choose storage based on your actual needs

More storage products are not always the answer. Before buying organizers, identify what needs to be stored, measure the available space and choose a solution that fits your routine.

How to Keep Your Home Organized

The most useful organization system is one that you can maintain. Keep frequently used items accessible, return things to their designated places and regularly remove items that are no longer useful.

You do not need to organize your entire home at once. Starting with one problem area can make the process easier and give you a system that can be repeated in other rooms.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the easiest way to start organizing a home?

Start with one small area that causes daily frustration, such as a kitchen drawer, closet shelf or entryway. Remove unnecessary items and give the remaining items clear storage locations.

How can I organize a small home without adding lots of furniture?

Focus on vertical storage, under-bed storage, cabinet organizers, hooks and other solutions that use existing space efficiently.

Should I buy storage products before decluttering?

It is usually helpful to sort and measure first. This lets you understand what actually needs to be stored before choosing containers or organizers.

Final Thoughts

A well-organized home is not necessarily a perfectly tidy home. The goal is to create simple systems that make everyday life easier.

Start small, use the space you already have and choose organization methods that fit the way you actually live.

Privacy Policy

Introduction

HomeNest respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit our website, how that information may be used and how cookies and advertising technologies may operate on the site.

Information We Collect

HomeNest may collect information that you voluntarily provide, such as information submitted through a contact form or email.

We may also receive limited technical information automatically when you visit the website, such as browser type, device information, approximate location, pages visited and general website usage information. This information may be used to understand how visitors use the website and to improve our content and website experience.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

HomeNest may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality, understand visitor activity and support advertising and measurement services.

Cookies are small data files that may be stored on your device when you visit a website. Some cookies may be necessary for website functionality, while others may be used for analytics, advertising or other purposes.

Advertising

HomeNest may use third-party advertising services, including Google AdSense, to display advertisements.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

Advertising technologies may also be used to measure advertising performance and help provide relevant advertisements.

Users may manage or opt out of personalized advertising through Google's advertising settings and other available privacy controls.

Privacy Choices and Consent

Depending on your location, you may be presented with a consent or privacy message when using HomeNest. These settings may allow you to make choices about cookies, personalized advertising and the use of certain information.

Privacy and advertising requirements may differ depending on where you are located. HomeNest may use appropriate consent and privacy tools when required for visitors in applicable regions.

Third-Party Services and Links

HomeNest may use third-party services for advertising, website functionality, analytics or other purposes. These third parties may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable settings.

Our website may also contain links to third-party websites. HomeNest is not responsible for the privacy practices, security or content of external websites. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of those websites separately.

Children's Privacy

HomeNest is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to maintain the security of information handled through the website. However, no method of transmission or storage over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our Contact page.
